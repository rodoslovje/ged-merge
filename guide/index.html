GED Merge User's Guide

GED Merge compares, edits, and merges GEDCOM family tree files entirely in your browser — nothing is uploaded. This guide covers the four main modes: Edit, Merge, Tools, and the underlying concepts.

Main Concepts

Master & Incoming

The Master file is your primary family tree. The Incoming file is the tree you want to compare and merge into the Master — a second GEDCOM file, or a matches CSV exported from indeks.rodoslovje.si.

Matching & Scoring

The app automatically compares individuals and families between the two files. It calculates a match score (0–100%) based on similarities in names, dates, places, and relationships. Kinship distance from a chosen home person is used to rank candidates so your closest relatives surface first.

Edit — Editing Your Master GEDCOM

Overview

Switch to Edit mode to browse and directly modify your Master GEDCOM without needing a second file. Select any person from the jump-to menu at the top, or navigate by clicking a family member's card. Use the Back button to return to your previous person. If the current person also has a match in your incoming file, click Merge in the toolbar to jump straight to that comparison. All changes stay in memory until you download the file, and can be stepped back with Undo / Redo (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z) in the header.

Names & Identity

Given name, surname, sex, and nickname are all editable inline — click a field, type, then click away to save. Use the × button to clear a field. Click + Add Name to attach alternative name variants (married, name at birth, aka, formal, and more); each variant has its own given name, surname, and type selector. Remove a variant with the − button.

Events

Birth is always shown. Click + Add Event to reveal additional events grouped by life stage:
  • Early Life: Baptism, Christening, Confirmation, Adoption, First Communion
  • Career: Occupation, Education, Retirement
  • Residence & Movement: Residence, Emigration, Immigration, Naturalization, Census
  • Estate: Will, Probate
  • Death: Death, Burial, Cremation
Each event row has date, place, address, and note fields, plus optional per-event links. Use × to clear a field, or the × at the end of the row to remove the entire event.

Family Relationships

The edit panel shows three family sections:
  • Parents: Father and mother from each parent family. Use + Add Father / + Add Mother to search for an existing person in your tree or add a new one. The − icon detaches the role without deleting the person's record.
  • Partners: Use + Add Partner to create a new spouse family the same way — search existing or add new. Each spouse family shows the marriage event and lets you add Engagement, Separation, or Divorce via + Add Family Event. The − icon detaches the spouse without deleting them. Families also support their own notes and links.
  • Children: Listed under each spouse family. Use + Add Child to search for an existing person or add a new one, or the − icon to unlink a child from that family without deleting them.

Notes & Sources

Both individuals and families can carry free-text notes and URL links. Click + Add Note or + Add Source to reveal the input fields. Once a link is entered, an ↗ icon opens it in a new tab. Click × to remove any note or link.

Removing Records

Detach (the − icon) only removes the family connection — both individual records remain intact and can be re-linked later.

Delete Person (the trash icon at the bottom of the panel) permanently removes the individual and unlinks them from every family they belong to. A confirmation dialog appears before deletion. This cannot be undone — download a backup first if needed.

Saving Your Work

Click Save GEDCOM to open a preview listing how many persons and families were changed. Click Download GEDCOM to save the edited file to your computer. Your original loaded file is never modified.

Merge — Compare & Merge Workflow

Reviewing Differences

Load a second GEDCOM (or a matches CSV from indeks.rodoslovje.si) as the Incoming file. Select a match from the list to see a side-by-side comparison of their data. Only fields with new data or conflicts are highlighted.

Compare Tree

Click Compare Tree to visually inspect the ancestors and descendants of a matched person to ensure they are exactly who you think they are. Click Edit next to it to jump straight to editing the master person in Edit mode.

Resolving Conflicts

For each conflicting field, choose whether to keep the Master value (M), take the Incoming value (I), or keep both (B).

Marking for Merge

Use the decision buttons or keyboard shortcuts to categorize each match as Confirmed (C), Rejected (R), or Deferred (D).

How Merging Works

When you click Save GEDCOM, the app opens a preview of all confirmed matches and edits. Click Download GEDCOM there to apply your field-level choices to a copy of the Master file and download it — seamlessly inserting any new individuals or families from the Incoming file that are connected to those confirmed matches.

Your original files remain completely untouched; the merged file is downloaded to your computer securely.

Tools — Maintenance Tools

The Tools tab runs whole-file checks and clean-ups on your Master GEDCOM. All five tools work entirely in your browser — no data is sent to a server.

Health Check

Scans every individual and family for common data problems: missing names or sex, no vital dates, broken internal links, future-dated events, death before birth, and disconnected records not linked to any family. Issues are grouped by category so you can navigate and fix them one by one in Edit mode.

Find Duplicates

Compares every person in the file against every other person using the same scoring engine as the Merge mode. Likely duplicate pairs are listed with their similarity score. Click a name to open it in Edit, or expand a pair to see a side-by-side field comparison before deciding whether to merge or dismiss.

Normalize

Detects the dominant date format, place layout, and link style in your file, then re-renders every record that deviates from it. Typical corrections include reformatting dates (e.g. 1 Jan 1900 → 01.01.1900), reshaping structured place fields, and rewriting Matricula or Geneanet links to a consistent form. A preview lists examples of each change; the original file is untouched until you download the normalized copy.

Sources

Displays all repositories, source records, and media objects in the file, grouped by repository. Clicking a source or media entry shows which individuals and families cite it, with direct links to open each record in Edit. Unattached media and links with no associated source are listed separately.

Places

Lists every place name used in the file, arranged in a country → locality → house → address hierarchy. Each place shows how many records use it. Click a place to see which records refer to it and open them in Edit. Use the Rename action on any level to bulk-rename or merge a place segment across every record that mentions it — useful for standardizing spelling variations.

Privacy

All parsing, matching, merging, and editing happens locally in your browser via Web Workers. Your family tree is never sent to a server.
