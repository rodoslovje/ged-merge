You are responsible for having the right to process what is in your file, and for its safekeeping. Keep backups. A merge, a bulk normalization or a clean-up tool rewrites many records at once; work on a copy and check the change report before replacing your working file.

Living people. Family trees usually contain personal data about people who are alive. In your own private research that is generally a household activity outside the GDPR's scope, but the exemption is narrow: publishing a chart, hosting a tree online or sending a file to someone else can make you a data controller with the duties that follow.
